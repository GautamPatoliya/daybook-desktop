QUICK ANSWERS

Will this delete my tasks?

No. It's a visual theme only — your data stays exactly as-is.

How do I update if I'm already on Daybook?

Open the app → Updates tab → Check for updates → Restart & install. Takes about 1 minute.

Can I switch back to the old look?

Yes — Settings → Profile → pick Default anytime.
